# One Variety per Bed

A Bed has one Bed plan and one Variety. Tomato with basil at its feet is two neighboring Beds, not one interplanted Bed.

Companion planting is real horticulture and the obvious “fix” later. It would split watering, harvest, and replant Care events across two plants that share a name. Suggestions stay “stronger Fit, same Kind,” not “plant these together.”
