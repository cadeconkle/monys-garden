# Exhaustive Catalog, filtered by Fit

The Catalog is not a shortlist. It aims to include every Variety that can be grown at the Growing place. The Gardener finds her way with Fit (strong to weak candidate) and Suggestions to other Varieties.

The alternative was a small, fully finished starter set she could request into. That is easier to keep accurate and photoreal. She asked for the whole field of candidates, with honesty about which ones are weak, so a peach that merely survives here still appears — it just ranks weak next to one that thrives.
