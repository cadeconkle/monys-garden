# Mony's Garden

The household garden in Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina, and the plants, kitchen uses, and care work that belong to it.

## Place and people

**Growing place**:
Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina — the only climate this garden is written for.
_Avoid_: location, region, site

**Garden**:
The household's planted space around the house at the Growing place: front, side, back, and patio. The kitchen is not a garden; plants may be brought indoors for a time.
_Avoid_: landscape, plot, kitchen garden

**Gardener**:
Mony — the only person this app is for.
_Avoid_: user, owner, wife

## Plants

**Category**:
A top-level browse group: fruit trees, herbs, vegetables, flowers, shrubs, bushes, trees.
_Avoid_: type, class

**Kind**:
A common plant the Gardener would name in conversation (tomato, Thai basil, peach). The browse rung between Category and Variety.
_Avoid_: species, crop

**Variety**:
A named cultivar she would buy or seed. What a Catalog page and a Planting both point at.
_Avoid_: plant (overloaded), species

**Kitchen tradition**:
Asian, American, or both — the cooking the Gardener uses a Variety for. Only for Varieties grown to eat or use in the kitchen.
_Avoid_: style, Asian style, American style

**Ornamental**:
A Variety grown for how it looks in the yard, not for the kitchen. Beauty is American-yard only; this is not an Asian ornamental garden catalog.
_Avoid_: decoration, beauty plant, landscaping plant

## Planted space

**Area**:
One of Front, Side, Back, Patio, or Indoors — a part of the Garden. Indoors is a holding place, not a garden.
_Avoid_: zone, yard

**Bed**:
A named spot inside an Area: an in-ground bed, a row, a pot, or a group of pots.
_Avoid_: plot, container, spot

**Planting**:
A Variety actually being grown in a Bed.
_Avoid_: instance, entry, crop
