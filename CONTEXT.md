# Mony's Garden

The household garden in Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina, and the plants, kitchen uses, and care work that belong to it.

## Language

**Growing place**:
Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina — the only climate this garden is written for.
_Avoid_: location, region, area, site

**Garden**:
The household's planted outdoor space at the Growing place, including kitchen plantings and the front yard.
_Avoid_: landscape, plot
