# Uneven Catalog finish

The Catalog is listed-exhaustive on day one, not finished-exhaustive. Every Variety ships with a name, Category, Kind, Fit, and a why. Photoreal renders and full care (soil, Winter fate, Difficulty, Harvest, Techniques, Buy place) land first on strong Fit and on anything the Gardener plants.

Waiting until every page is photoreal and fully researched would block the app. Hiding thin pages would fake a shortlist we already rejected. Fit is how she sees the unfinished edge.
